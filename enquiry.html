Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes do your fragrances come in?

Our fragrances are available in 40ml, 80ml and 100ml bottles, depending on the specific scent. Please check the Products page for the exact size and price of each fragrance.

How long does a bottle usually last?

This depends on how often you wear the fragrance and how many sprays you use per application, but most 40ml bottles last several weeks with daily wear.

Are your fragrances suitable for sensitive skin?

We recommend doing a small patch test on your skin before full application if you have sensitive skin or known allergies.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we offer delivery. Please contact us directly for delivery areas, timelines and costs.

Can I return or exchange a fragrance?

Please contact us as soon as possible if there is an issue with your order so we can assist you with a return or exchange.

How do I place an order?

You can place an order by contacting us via email, phone, WhatsApp or Instagram. See our Contact page for details.

Have another question?

If you couldn't find the answer you were looking for, please reach out to us directly on our Contact page and we will be happy to help.
