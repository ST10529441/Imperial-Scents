Some Helpfull tips

How to Choose Your Scent

Choosing the right fragrance starts with understanding fragrance families. Fresh scents are light and citrusy, perfect for daytime. Woody scents are warm and grounding, ideal for evenings. Floral scents are romantic and feminine, while oriental scents are rich and spicy. Test a fragrance on your skin and let it settle for 30 minutes before deciding. Browse our fragrances.

Fragrance Tips

Apply fragrance to pulse points such as your wrists, neck, and behind your ears. Do not rub your wrists together as this breaks down the scent molecules. Layer your fragrance with matching body lotion for longer-lasting results. Store your bottles in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight.

Scent Care 101

Keep your perfume bottles tightly closed when not in use. Avoid storing them in the bathroom where heat and humidity can degrade the fragrance. Most perfumes last 3 to 5 years if stored properly. If the colour or smell changes, it may be time to replace it.
