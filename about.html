Our Brief History

Imperial Scents was established on 29 July 2026 by Ofentse Lebenya as a sole proprietorship with a clear purpose: to make premium-quality fragrances accessible to everyone. As a newly launched business, Imperial Scents enters the market with fresh energy, a customer-first mindset and a commitment to breaking down the barriers that often separate luxury scents from everyday consumers. Despite being in its early stages, the business is already projecting a sustainable monthly profit, signalling strong potential for growth.

Our Mission Statement

To provide affordable, high-quality scents paired with remarkable service that delights our current clients and welcomes future ones into the Imperial Scents family.

Our Vision Statement

To become South Africa's most trusted and accessible fragrance destination where luxury meets affordability and every customer feels valued.

Our Story, Our Journey

Every bottle we sell carries a little bit of our own journey with it. Imperial Scents was born from a simple belief: that everyone deserves to feel like royalty, without paying a royal price. From students looking for their first signature scent, to young professionals building their personal brand, to loved ones searching for the perfect gift, we built this brand for you. We are still growing, still learning and still listening, and we would love for you to be part of the Imperial Scents family as we write the next chapter of our story together.
